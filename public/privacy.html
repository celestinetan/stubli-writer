Privacy and terms

Your notes never reach us. Stubli Writer has no server, no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Where your notes live

A folder on your computer. Your browser reads and writes the Markdown files in the folder you choose. They don't go anywhere else.

Google Drive. Your notes go in a “Stubli Writer” folder in your own Drive. Stubli Writer asks Google only for permission to see and change the files it makes itself, never the rest of your Drive. Your browser talks to Google directly; nothing passes through us. Google's own privacy policy applies to your Drive.

Signing in to Google lasts about an hour and is kept only in the open page's memory. Nothing that lets anyone into your Drive is stored on your device.

What's kept on this device

  • Your settings: font, page colour, accent, and where your notes live.
  • With Google Drive: a copy of your notes and any changes waiting to upload, so you can write without a connection.
  • A copy of the app itself, so it opens without a connection.

To remove them, choose Change at the bottom of the app, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings. Notes in your folder or your Drive stay where they are.

What the app contacts

Only Google, and only when you use Google Drive: accounts.google.com to sign in and www.googleapis.com to read and save your notes. Pictures in your notes that live on other websites aren't loaded, so those sites can't tell when you read a note. The page's security policy blocks every other address.

Stubli Writer's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: it is used only to show and save your own notes, for you.
